## 5. BA1-T2 - split-or-collapse pressure test

The semantic one-family collapse was **REJECTED** because referent identity and proposition identity/origin/evolution remain independently required.

### Minimality refinement

R1 allowed `BAProposition` to be a project-semantic target of another proposition. T2 found this unnecessary and potentially misleading.

The refined boundary is:

- project meaning that needs independent reuse/qualification -> `BAReferent`;
- methodology-neutral assertion about project meaning -> `BAProposition`;
- assertion provenance/diagnostic/change metadata -> BA3 mechanisms targeting proposition identity.

The active candidate is now `BA1_MINIMAL_BAE_ONTOLOGY_CANDIDATE_R2.md`.

## 6. BA1-T3 - minimal BAE ontology closure review

**NEXT / NOT EXECUTED BY THIS PLAN REVISION.**

BA1-T3 is a closure/falsification review, not another ontology invention exercise.

It must test:

1. **Necessity:** does removing either `BAReferent` or `BAProposition` break a closed BA0 responsibility or observed corpus requirement?
2. **Sufficiency:** does the two-family boundary cover the tested corpora without hiding a still-unresolved first-class identity responsibility?
3. **No leakage:** can the boundary be stated without relation vocabulary, document types, STRIDE/DFD categories, ThreatForge classes, view/tool/storage semantics or general systems-modeling taxonomies?
4. **Correct phase placement:** do predicates, roles and actions belong to BA2; provenance and identity mechanics to BA3; projections to BA4; vocabulary assistance to BA5; and regression to BA6?
5. **Minimality refinement:** is proposition-as-project-target correctly excluded from the required core?
6. **Stop criterion:** if no material counterexample remains and unresolved details belong later, close BA1 rather than invent BA1-T4 for reassurance.
