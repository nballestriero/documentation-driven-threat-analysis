\subsection{6. BA2-T4 - relation/action vocabulary closure
review}\label{6-ba2-t4---relationaction-vocabulary-closure-review}

\textbf{NEXT / NOT EXECUTED BY THIS PLAN REVISION.}

BA2-T4 must review the regressed R2 candidate without expanding
vocabulary unless a concrete closure counterexample appears.

It must decide:

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  whether the thirteen-key operator seed is sufficient and minimal for
  current thesis scope;
\item
  whether the operator-role/cardinality matrix is coherent and does not
  hide method-specific assumptions;
\item
  whether
  \texttt{ownOrManage\ -\textgreater{}\ assignResponsibility\ +\ responsibilityKind\ +\ polarity}
  preserves the needed responsibility boundary;
\item
  whether explicit polarity plus only local \texttt{condition} and
  \texttt{temporalScope} modifiers is sufficient;
\item
  whether atomicity/concurrency/idempotency/completion and similar
  governed rules belong in \texttt{constrain} rather than modifiers;
\item
  whether classification-as-proposition plus a controlled semantic-kind
  registry contract is sufficient without a fixed universal taxonomy;
\item
  whether semantic-key/lexical-label separation is clean enough to defer
  labels/synonyms to BA5;
\item
  whether any remaining issue actually belongs to BA3 rather than BA2;
\item
  whether any concrete counterexample triggers BA1 reopen.
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{BA2-T4 exit condition}\label{ba2-t4-exit-condition}

Close BA2 only if no material relation/action/role/modifier
counterexample remains and all unresolved items can be allocated
honestly to BA3/BA5/BA6 without weakening mechanical analysis
consumption.

If a concrete counterexample appears, reopen only the smallest affected
BA2 candidate responsibility. Do not broaden into general systems
modeling.
