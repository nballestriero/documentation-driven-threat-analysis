## Closed BA1 ontology

BA1 accepts exactly two first-class semantic identity families:

```text
BAReferent     ACCEPTED
BAProposition  ACCEPTED
```

`BAE` remains an umbrella term and is not required as an additional metaclass.

`BAReferent` identifies reusable methodology-neutral shared project meaning. `BAProposition` identifies methodology-neutral analytical assertions about BAReferents so claim-level origin/provenance, diagnosis and change disposition can remain distinct from referent identity.

A BAReferent is an analytical semantic referent and is not limited to one runtime object instance. Behavior/event, information/resource, participant/capability, store, contract, boundary and state/context meanings may all receive BAReferent identity when independently reusable.

BA1 does **not** require those categories to become dedicated first-class metaclasses. Method-neutral classifications/roles may distinguish them; exact relation, participation-role and classification mechanics remain downstream.

## Closed split criterion

A future first-class BAE split requires both:

1. independent semantic identity across propositions/projections/change; and
2. reusable subtype-specific invariants that cannot be represented honestly as classifications, roles, values or propositions over BAReferent/BAProposition.

Implementation convenience, diagram categories and method-specific taxonomies are insufficient.
