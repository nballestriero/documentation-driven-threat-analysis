# Human-Readable and Thesis Output Targets

R16 makes the end products explicit so that intermediate reviews collect the evidence needed to publish them without reconstructing decisions from chat history.

## A. New normative Base Analysis methodology

After all operator, condition-language, and pressure reviews are complete:

- publish a new version of the BA construction methodology;
- integrate only deltas that pass G1–G8;
- document backward compatibility with BA2 R3;
- include migration notes where signatures or authoring forms change;
- keep documentation-authoring methodology out of scope.

## B. Updated Base Analysis operational/authoring guide

The guide must be readable by an analyst who did not participate in the research process. It should include:

- term-by-term explanations;
- source-to-BA extraction examples;
- positive and rejected examples;
- human-readable notation first, machine structure second;
- explicit non-inference rules;
- operator-selection decision aids;
- traceability and QA examples.

## C. Complete DermaTriage Base Analysis

Produce both:

1. a human-readable review document;
2. a deterministic machine-readable representation suitable for reproducibility and projections.

The human-readable document should expose, at minimum:

- source anchor and evidence-bearing wording;
- BAReferents and semantic kinds;
- BAPropositions in readable notation;
- decision rules and conditions;
- process/pipeline/interface/boundary/store relations actually supported by the final method;
- non-inferences and intentional omissions;
- documentation QA findings kept separate from BA truth;
- end-to-end traceability back to the governed source.

## D. Before/after case-study comparison

Compare the pre-review BA with the final re-run and identify:

- meaning recovered more faithfully;
- information previously lost or flattened;
- prior awkward representations corrected;
- excessive inference removed;
- remaining documentation gaps;
- constructs that proved unnecessary or not tested.

## E. Thesis-update pack

Preserve thesis-ready material for:

- evolution of the BA methodology;
- empirical rationale for each accepted/rejected delta;
- validation methodology and anti-overfitting controls;
- DermaTriage case-study results;
- DDTA strengths and weaknesses;
- how BA extraction exposes documentation ambiguity or missing bindings;
- limitations and future validation needs.

The thesis pack should cite repository checkpoints and governed source anchors so that the written thesis can be updated from stable evidence rather than conversational recollection.
