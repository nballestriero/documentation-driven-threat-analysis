## Four reading questions

### 1. Research problem and contribution

The abstract reports an extension to the RESCUE process that manually applies patterns to i* system models in order to generate textual requirement statements for a complex air-traffic-control system.

### 2. Starting artifacts and system representation

The starting artifact is an already-developed i* system model; patterns are applied to the model, not directly to heterogeneous source documentation.

### 3. Traceability, change, automation and human role

The abstract states that application was manual and future/ongoing work aimed to implement the patterns in REDEPEND. Detailed traceability and review procedures cannot be assessed without full text.

### 4. Findings, limitations and relationship with DDTA / overlay

The abstract supports the existence of a bridge from formal/semi-formal models to textual requirements, but not its detailed quality, coverage or generality. DDTA must not infer model-to-text generation rules from the abstract alone.
