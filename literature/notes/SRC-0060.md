## Four reading questions

### 1. Research problem and contribution

This very short note proposes dimensions/criteria for reasoning about genres of applications/domains. It is explicitly presented as initial ideas rather than as a validated taxonomy.

### 2. Starting artifacts and system representation

The dimensions include ownership, timescale, physical/social constraints, degrees of requirements freedom, development pathways, locus of design, solution dimensions and maturity of domain knowledge. They are intended as 'tools for thought' and are not necessarily orthogonal.

### 3. Traceability, change, automation and human role

No automation, tool or formal matching process is reported. The contribution is a conceptual set of dimensions for analysts.

### 4. Findings, limitations and relationship with DDTA / overlay

For DDTA, the note is useful because it suggests that discrimination may be multi-dimensional rather than a single named class. Its evidential weight is modest: two pages, workshop context, explicitly provisional, and calling for a corpus/refinement.
