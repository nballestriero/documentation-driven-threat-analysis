## Four reading questions

### 1. Research problem and contribution

Publisher material presents a consistent vocabulary/visual notation and a catalogue of 65 recurring messaging/integration solutions. It is a solution/integration pattern language rather than a problem-domain classification system.

### 2. Starting artifacts and system representation

The source starts from integration design concerns—channels, messages, routing, transformation, endpoints and management. These are software/integration architecture structures.

### 3. Traceability, change, automation and human role

Patterns are applied by architects/designers; no requirements authority or documentation extraction mechanism is indicated in the reviewed material.

R23 can use this as a clean downstream comparator for pattern language/vocabulary. It must not be used to infer that a messaging pattern describes governed project semantics. A separately registered 2002 Hohpe PLoP paper provides legal full-text evidence for the precursor pattern language.
