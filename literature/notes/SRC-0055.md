## Four reading questions

### 1. Research problem and contribution

The publisher preview establishes Fowler's focus on reusable conceptual/object models that recur across domains, rather than on a modelling process alone. Fowler explicitly positions the first part as conceptual business-model patterns and separates support patterns that connect conceptual models to software architecture/implementation.

### 2. Starting artifacts and system representation

The accessible introduction/preface starts from modelling experience and recurrent conceptual structures in domains such as trading, measurement, accounting and organisations. Patterns include reasoning, applicability/non-applicability rules and alternatives. The full pattern catalogue was not reviewed.

### 3. Traceability, change, automation and human role

The source is a human modelling catalogue, not an automated matching system. The publisher material does not support claims about provenance, lifecycle change or automatic generation.

### 4. Findings, limitations and relationship with DDTA / overlay

For R23, the most useful previewed distinction is conceptual analysis pattern versus support/implementation concerns. This is evidence that reusable semantic models can be a different level from GoF design patterns, but it is not evidence for a complete problem-class recognition mechanism.
