# Literature classification taxonomy

A source may have one primary topic and multiple secondary topics.

## TM — Threat modeling
- `TM-GENERAL`: principles, processes, and comparative methods
- `TM-STRIDE`: STRIDE and DFD-oriented analysis
- `TM-PRIVACY`: LINDDUN and privacy threat modeling
- `TM-AUTOMATION`: automated or model-supported threat identification
- `TM-CONTINUOUS`: evolving, versioned, or DevSecOps-integrated threat models
- `TM-AI-SYSTEMS`: threat modeling for AI/ML/LLM systems

## RE — Requirements engineering
- `RE-SECURITY`: security requirements elicitation and derivation
- `RE-MISUSE`: misuse cases, abuse cases, anti-goals
- `RE-TRACEABILITY`: trace links across lifecycle artifacts
- `RE-PRE`: pre-requirements and rationale traceability
- `RE-NLP-LLM`: NLP or LLM support for requirements work

## MD — Model-driven engineering
- `MD-MDE`: model-driven software engineering
- `MD-MDS`: model-driven security
- `MD-KG`: knowledge graphs, semantic models, and graph-based traceability
- `MD-VIEW`: projections, views, and derived representations

## DE — Documentation and knowledge
- `DE-DOCS-AS-CODE`: version-controlled and executable documentation
- `DE-ARCH`: architecture documentation and decision records
- `DE-KNOWLEDGE`: governed project knowledge and provenance
- `DE-QUALITY`: completeness, consistency, freshness, and analyzability

## CS — Continuous security
- `CS-DEVSECOPS`: security integrated into delivery workflows
- `CS-CHANGE`: change impact and stale-analysis detection
- `CS-ASSURANCE`: evidence, verification, and assurance arguments
- `CS-HUMAN`: expert review and human-in-the-loop governance

## DDTA relevance roles
- `foundation`: defines a concept DDTA directly adopts or refines
- `neighbor`: solves an adjacent part of the pipeline
- `comparison`: plausible closest related approach
- `evaluation`: informs research design or metrics
- `context`: provides broader background
- `challenge`: exposes a limitation or alternative interpretation
