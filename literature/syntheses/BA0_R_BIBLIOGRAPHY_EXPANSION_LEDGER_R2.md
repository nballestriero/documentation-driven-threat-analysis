## Bounded-search decision

BA0-R9 closes the planned *alternative semantic-kernel challenge*: OPM provides the missing compact ontology counterexample. No additional second-ring source should be added before BA0 unless one of two conditions occurs:

1. the user-provided ISO 42010 full text materially changes view/viewpoint conclusions; or
2. BA0/BA1 produces a concrete unresolved semantic question that the current corpus cannot answer.

This prevents bibliography expansion from becoming an unbounded substitute for metamodel falsification.
