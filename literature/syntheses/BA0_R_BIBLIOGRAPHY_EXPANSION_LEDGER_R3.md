## Bounded-search decision

The current corpus is sufficient to enter BA0. No additional source is mandatory before BA0 unless a concrete downstream counterexample requires it. The 2011 full-text ISO regression changes the view/projection conclusion enough to warrant the Revision 3 synthesis, but it does not produce a Chapter 3/4 reopen trigger or a need for further open-ended literature expansion.
