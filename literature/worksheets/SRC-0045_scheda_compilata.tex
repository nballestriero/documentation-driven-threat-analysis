\section{1. Research problem and contribution}
The Open Group describes ArchiMate as an independent enterprise-architecture modeling language. Official access requires accepting a personal or organizational license. An authoritative Open Group Community guide provides a practical non-normative introduction to its generic metamodel.
\section{2. Starting artifacts and generated representations}
The open guide distinguishes Active Structure, Behavior and Passive Structure. Passive structure represents stocks/information/goods acted upon by behavior. It distinguishes Flow, Triggering and Serving relationship semantics and then specializes the generic concepts into business, application and technology layers.
\section{3. Traceability, change, automation and human role}
The reviewed open guide emphasizes viewpoints/views and a constrained metamodel but is not a lifecycle/governance specification. No DDTA-style provenance conclusions should be attributed to the inaccessible normative 3.2 text.
\section{4. Findings, limitations and relationship with DDTA}
The generic Active/Behavior/Passive split is a strong independent challenge to Asset as a root and Interaction as one universal relation. It suggests “asset” is a security interpretation of more neutral passive/information objects.
